Privacy Policy

Basbousa Studio Personal is a private, single-user tool that produces short animated videos and uploads them to the owner's own social accounts.

Data we handle

  • Basic profile information and an access token for the owner's own TikTok account, used only to upload the owner's own videos.
  • Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer in the operating system credential store. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

What we do not do

No data from other users is collected. No advertising, tracking, or analytics profiles are built. The tool is not offered to the public.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings and are deleted locally on request.
